# Reviewer suggestions — for user review before submission

   reproducibility in neural simulation; ideal for the determinism
   and corpus-contribution assessment.
2. **Keri Ryan or Ian Meinertzhagen** (Dalhousie) — Ciona connectome
   upstream author; can judge dataset representation fidelity.
   *(Check conflict rules — upstream authors are sometimes excluded.)*
3. **Marcus Walfish** (NYU) — verifiable-computation framing; authored
   the survey we position against.
4. **Joshua Vogelstein** (JHU) — connectomics tooling/graph
   statistics; mesoscale/cellular distinction scrutiny.
5. **Andrew Hill** (Oxford) or AudioMoth/ecoacoustics authors — the
   ecological-sensing/MRV half.
6. A **PCI Ecology recommender** in biodiversity MRV — useful even
   pre-submission since PCJ is the fallback.

## Opposed reviewers (if asked)

- None on record. Consider opposing direct competitors only if the
  portal requires entries — e.g. authors whose prior critique of
  "connectome compute" claims was methodological.

## Portal notes

- *Neuroinformatics* submission: springer.com Editorial Manager;
  asks for ORCID, suggested reviewers (3+), classification terms,
  "methods" article type.
- PCI Ecology fallback: post on any open archive PCI accepts
  (bioRxiv, EcoEvoRxiv, arXiv, HAL, Zenodo), then "Submit preprint" at
  ecology.peercommunityin.org. PCI asks for **5–10 suggested
  recommenders** (their editor equivalents) and requires all
  data/code/script DOIs (Zenodo) at submission — the WiredReplay-1
  Zenodo DOI must exist before PCI submission. Recommended preprints
  transfer to Peer Community Journal (diamond OA, Scopus/WoS indexed)
  or stay as PCI-recommended preprints. bioRxiv also offers a direct
  B2J "PCI Ecology" route at the end of its submission form.
