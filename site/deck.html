the living cloud04

The consent contract

Four rules, enforced in code

1 · We never attach

No sensors on plants or animals, nothing in soil. Listening from a distance is the default.

2 · Nothing is hidden

Every reading and answer gets a receipt anyone can replay.

3 · Nature has a voice

Each ecosystem has a human guardian with custody. Refusal is a first-class value — the API says "no" out loud.

4 · Two yeses to write

Writes need guardian + owner consent, stay minimally disruptive, and refused lanes (hop / compute / engineered) refuse outright.

Not poster promises — the lane gate (lane-gate.rego · offers.scm) refuses what breaks them.

the living cloud05

Why nature

Green improves. Grey decays.

Machines wear out

Every grey solution degrades — silicon ages, buildings depreciate, batteries die.

Living systems heal

Forests self-repair, self-power, and get better at surviving every year — for free.

proven by science  Sparse biological wiring beats dense compute at scale — measured: medulla (40k neurons) 1.17e-1 J vs dense fp32 8.30 J (~70×). The degree-matched shuffle control refuted our own flattering interpretation — and we published it.

Ecosystems are already worth more alive — air, water, soil. If they also join humanity's knowledge work, the alignment deepens.
