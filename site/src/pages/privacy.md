## What we store

The only personal data this site collects is what you type into the waitlist form:

- your email address
- the role you pick (guardian, host, funder, scientist, curious)
- an optional place name
- a timestamp

It is stored in Cloudflare Workers KV (Treetable's account) under a **hashed key** —
the email itself is never used as a lookup key and is never listed or returned by any endpoint.

## What we do with it

Occasional emails when things actually happen — field pilots opening, major
milestones. No marketing drip, no sharing, no sale, no analytics on this site.

## What we don't do

- No third-party analytics or tracking pixels
- No advertising identifiers
- The receipt ledger (`/receipts`) contains only computation receipts — no personal data

## Removal

Email <contact@treetable.org> with the address you registered and it is deleted
from the waitlist within 30 days.

## Everything else

The rest of the system stores content-addressed ecological observations and
computation receipts — data about places and organisms, not about you.
