# USDA SBIR (NIFA) Phase I — watch draft (~$175k)

Status: FY26 Phase I window appears closed (the current NOFO is
Phase II only — requires a prior Phase I award). Watch for the FY27
Phase I RFA; prep is done here so it's a same-week submission.

## Topic fit (from nifa.usda.gov SBIR/STTR topic areas)

- **8.7 Aquaculture** — non-invasive tank/pond monitoring; C4D biofilm
  readout, hydrophone lanes map directly to aquaculture sensing needs
- **Forests and Related Resources** — the forest-listening cell
- **Plant Production and Protection** — E2 ultrasonic plant-stress
  clicks (natural dry-down, never induced)
- **Management of Natural Resources** — MRV/provenance angle
- **Small and Mid-Size Farms** — the $40-node affordability story is
  the exact buyer

## Angle vs. the NSF version

USDA reviews weight *agricultural application* and producer benefit —
lead with the farm/aquaculture deployment, not the MRV market.
The consent-gated, non-invasive posture plays well for animal-welfare
adjacent reviewers ("nothing attaches, nothing enters the habitat").
