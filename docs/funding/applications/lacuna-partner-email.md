**Subject:** Lacuna Fund partnership — receipted bioacoustic datasets (proposal co-lead?)

Dear SiB Colombia team (attn: Ricardo Ortiz Gallego),

Following on the GBIF BID thread — a second funding lane I think fits
your lab even better: **Lacuna Fund** grants for creating open ML
datasets in low- and middle-income countries. Their calls require a
lead institution headquartered in the region — yours qualifies; ours
(Treetable LLC, US) can only join as a technical partner.

**The idea.** Passive bioacoustic sensors generate huge labeled-data
potential, but labels die without provenance — a training set can't say
*how* each detection was evidenced. We build an open-source receipt
layer where every detection is content-addressed and replayable. The
Lacuna-shaped proposal: mobilize your existing/planned acoustic archives
into a **labeled, receipted eco-ML dataset** — usable for model training
*and* auditable end-to-end. The receipt layer becomes the dataset's
provenance backbone.

**Split.** Your lab: data mobilization + taxonomic QA (the funded core —
the money flows to the lead institution). Us: the receipt pipeline +
catalogue infrastructure, contributed.

**Why now.** Lacuna's agriculture/environment domain is active; a
receipted-dataset proposal is differentiated — nobody else can point to
a live, deployed provenance layer. If a dataset-creation RFP opens, we
can have a concept note ready inside a week.

Would co-developing a Lacuna proposal fit your roadmap — and shall I
send the two-page concept note?

Best,
T.R. Price
Temple Earth (Treetable LLC) · templeearth.cc · hello@templeearth.cc
