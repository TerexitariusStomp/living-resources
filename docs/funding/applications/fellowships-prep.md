# Fellowships & venture programs — prep notes (verify before applying)

Three programs worth watching; each needs a current-eligibility check
before any application claims. Reusable answers live in
`accelerators.md` + `concept-note.md`.

## Activate Fellowship

- **What:** 2-year fellowship for hard-tech founders — ~$100k+/yr living
  stipend + research funding + community. US-based, cohorts at
  Berkeley/Boston/NY/Anywhere.
- **Fit:** strong — hard tech, solo founder, pre-deployment is their
  exact profile. It's a *founder* fellowship (you, not the company).
- **Watch:** applications open ~annually (fall window typical).
  Requires full-time commitment to the company.

## Creative Destruction Lab (CDL)

- **What:** equity-free, objectives-based accelerator for seed-stage
  science/tech ventures. Relevant streams: Oceans (Halifax), Climate,
  Compute.
- **Fit:** CDL-Oceans is the natural stream — EcoCell marine lane +
  receipted MRV. Equity-free, but expects an investable venture;
  mentors assign hard 8-week objectives.
- **Watch:** annual cycle, applications ~spring/summer.

## Foresight Institute

- **What:** grants + fellowships for ambitious tech (bio, compute,
  nanotech). Tends toward researchers/nonprofits; check whether the
  LLC qualifies or whether the work needs a nonprofit wrapper.
- **Fit:** the organism-compute direction (preserved-wiring replay →
  gentle in-situ lanes) is their aesthetic, but present only what we
  actually claim — simulation over connectomes today.
- **Watch:** rolling/small grants; verify current program before
  writing prose.

## Common honest positioning for all three

- What exists is real: open stack, live receipted evidence layer,
  working board, 15-substrate replay-verified compute
- What's aspirational stays labeled: field deployment is funded-but-
  unproven; living compute is a research direction, not a claim
- The founder-strength story: built and shipped the whole thing solo
