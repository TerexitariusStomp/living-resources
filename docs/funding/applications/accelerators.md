# Accelerators — shared application draft

SOSV HAX (hardware), Elemental Impact (climate, $500k–5M deployments),
Carbon13 (UK climate venture builder), Katapult Ocean (if ocean lane
emphasized). Each asks variant questions; one reusable answer set.

## One-line

Receipted ecological sensing — every field reading carries a replayable
evidence chain.

## Team / traction

Open-source stack live (AGPL, working e2e suites), manufacturable node
(DRC-clean PCB), replay-verified compute layer over real organism
connectomes, measured efficiency benchmarks. Pre-revenue; revenue path
doesn't require hardware capital (verification services first).

## Climate/biodiversity impact

Monitoring is the bottleneck for nature action — you can't protect or
credit what you can't verifiably measure. Cheaper ($45 vs $600+) +
verifiable sensing unblocks biodiversity credits, protected-area
management, and climate MRV simultaneously.

"Solo founder, pre-deployment — the program's cofounder/team support
is part of why we're applying." Accelerators reward self-awareness;
the evidence artifacts (bench table, working code, real board files)
do the credibility work.
