# GBIF BID — fiscal host / partner outreach email

**To:** sib@humboldt.org.co — SiB Colombia / Instituto Humboldt (GBIF voting-participant node; already publishes AudioMoth→Arbimon→GBIF acoustic datasets)
**Subject:** GBIF BID partnership — receipted biodiversity data mobilization (€60k tier)

Dear SiB Colombia team (attn: Ricardo Ortiz Gallego, node manager),

I'm writing about a GBIF BID proposal I'd like to build with your lab as
the applying institution. BID grants require eligible institutional
applicants — the tech contribution would come from us.

**The idea in one paragraph.** Passive bioacoustic and environmental
sensors generate occurrence data that enters GBIF with almost no
provenance — a record says *what* was detected, not *how the detection
was evidenced*. We've built an open-source receipt layer (AGPL-3.0) where
every reading is content-addressed, witnessed by a quorum of independent
nodes, and replayable by any auditor. The proposal: a Receipt→Darwin Core
pipeline so mobilized records carry their evidence chain end-to-end,
plus a public receipt catalogue linked from the GBIF dataset pages.

**What your lab would contribute** (the BID-eligible part): data
mobilization and taxonomic QA on your existing or planned passive-sensing
datasets — we estimate ~€25k of the budget for that work, flowing to the
host. **What we'd contribute:** the receipt pipeline, catalogue
infrastructure, and (optionally) ~$35 sensing nodes for a small
deployment — ~€35k of the budget for the technical work.

**Why now.** GBIF's data-quality direction is moving toward machine-
actionable provenance; a receipted mobilization pipeline is a concrete,
reusable artifact other BID projects could adopt. It's the kind of
infrastructure the call is designed to fund.

Background on us: Treetable LLC builds receipted ecological sensing —
open stack live (e2e-verified sensing→quorum→receipt→settlement),
manufacturable low-cost field node, and a deployed replay-verified
compute API. Happy to send the working concept note (receipt→DwC
pipeline, budget split, deliverables) or jump on a short call.

Would this fit your lab's mobilization plans for the next BID window?

Best,

Treetable LLC · terexmaps.com · terexmaps@gmail.com
