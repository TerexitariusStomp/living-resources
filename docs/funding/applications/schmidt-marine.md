# Schmidt Marine Technology Partners — watch draft ($100–400k typical)

Status: the 2026 proposal window (June 1 – July 31) is **closed** —
this draft preps the next cycle. SMTP funds ocean technology at
**TRL 2–6** — exactly where EcoCell's marine lane sits. They fund
nonprofits primarily; a US LLC applies either via a nonprofit fiscal
partner or confirms current for-profit eligibility on the call.
Application: concept note first, then invited full proposal.

## Concept note skeleton (~2 pages, their format)

**The gap.** Ocean observing runs on expensive hardware and unverifiable
data. Moored and coastal sensors cost hundreds to thousands per node,
and every exported record loses its provenance — a detection says what
was found, not how it was evidenced.

**The technology.** An open-hardware marine sensing node (~$40 BOM):
hydrophone front-end, air mic, contactless C4D biofilm readout,
environmental sensing — every lane's electronics on every device. Passive
and non-invasive by design: nothing attaches, nothing moors, emission
lanes are consent-gated with logged duty cycles (marine-mammal-sensitive
deployments stay listen-only unless a guardian + operator jointly open
a lane).

**The evidence layer.** Each reading is content-addressed (SAID),
witnessed by a quorum of independent nodes, issued as a replayable
receipt. Detection provenance survives export — to GBIF, to portals,
to regulators. Already live: a public receipt ledger + replay-verified
compute API at templeearth.cc.

**TRL today.** TRL 3–4: working e2e suites, lab-validated board
(DRC-clean), packaged firmware, live cloud layer — the grant funds
TRL 5–6 (salt/fouling-hardened marine packaging + coastal field
deployment + blinded validation). Inside their band.
