## Project name
Temple Earth — the living cloud

## Tagline (short description)
Non-invasive ecological sensing where nature is a participant — every
field reading becomes a public, replay-verifiable receipt.

## About (main body)

Temple Earth is building a living cloud: small open-hardware listeners
sit *near* forests, reefs and soil — never touching, never attaching —
and the living world participates as a provider, with a guardian, a
consent gate, and a share.

**What already exists (not a promise):**
- An open-source (AGPL) stack: sensing → quorum → witnessed receipts
- A live public ledger — every compute task is replay-verifiable at
  templeearth.cc/receipts
- ~$40 universal field node: air mic, hydrophone, biofilm (C4D),
  environmental sensing on one board, Reticulum mesh radio
- Federation catalogue integrating 20+ allied ecological sensor
  networks with provenance intact
- Replay-verified computation over 15 real organism connectome wirings

**Why public goods:** the receipt layer, the adapter seam, the board
files, the protocol — all AGPL. Nobody has to trust us; the evidence
verifies itself.

**Where donations go:** field-node first-articles, E0 pilot deployment,
blinded validation, open documentation.
