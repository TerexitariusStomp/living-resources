## Concept note

### Title

Receipted biodiversity data mobilization: provenance-preserving
publication of passive sensing records

### Objective

Mobilize passive bioacoustic/environmental occurrence data into GBIF
with a receipt layer preserving per-reading provenance — so published
records carry the evidence chain (SAID → quorum → witnessed receipt)
that produced them. Partners: [university museum / NGO], us as tech.

### Deliverables

- Receipt→Darwin Core pipeline (occurrence records with provenance
  extensions)
- Partner deployment + mobilization of [N] records under CC-BY
- Public receipt catalogue linked from GBIF dataset pages
