# Gitcoin Regen Coordination round — submission draft

Quadratic funding rewards community over check size — spend effort on
the public story and mobilizing small donors, not a grant-ese wall.

## Project title

The living cloud: ecological subjects as participants, not commodities

## Short description (for the round page)

An open-source sensing commons where every field reading becomes a
witnessed, replayable receipt — and where the ecological subjects
themselves (through human guardians) earn per-contribution credit.
Plants, reefs, microbial mats and biofilms are treated as participants
with agency in a global network — not raw data sources. Non-invasive by
design: passive listening only, nothing attached, consent-gated.

## What your donation funds

- Field nodes anyone can build (~$40, all electronics on one open PCB)
- The receipt layer: SAIDs → quorum → witnessed receipts → credit
- Replay-verified compute over real organism connectomes
- The public catalogue where every claim links to its evidence chain

## Why quadratic funding fits

The living cloud IS a coordination public good — per-reading receipts
with mutual credit is the ReFi thesis implemented at the sensor level,
not the token level.

## Community assets to attach

- Repo link (working e2e suites, honest docs — reviewers can run tests)
- The measured bench table — including where we lose
- A live replay URL for a connectome task
- EcoCell board render + FIELD-KIT (shows it's real, not vapor)

"compute remains refused for living systems until evidence gates
graduate it — what we ship is simulation over preserved wiring, and we
