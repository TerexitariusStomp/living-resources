## Page body skeleton

### What it is

A universal ecological sensing node: air microphone, ultrasonic MEMS,
hydrophone/C4D front-end, light + temp/humidity/pressure sensors,
camera option, LoRa + Reticulum mesh store-and-forward, solar/battery
power management — all on ONE open-hardware PCB. Plus firmware that
turns every detection into a witnessed, replayable receipt.

### Why it exists

Biodiversity monitoring runs on $600+ proprietary loggers or DIY rigs
nobody can audit. Data buyers can't verify; field teams can't afford
fleet scale. EcoCell is $45 and every detection carries an evidence
chain a third party can replay.

### What makes it different

| | EcoCell One | Proprietary loggers | DIY rigs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cost | ~$45 | $300–1000+ | variable |
| Multimodal | audio+ultrasonic+hydro+env | usually one channel | DIY |
| Verifiable data | witnessed receipts, replayable | no | no |
| Open hardware | fully, AGPL | no | partial |
| Mesh backhaul | Reticulum/LXMF | cellular/Wi-Fi | DIY |
