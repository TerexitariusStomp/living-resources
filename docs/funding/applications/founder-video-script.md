# Founder intro video — script (~60 seconds)

Works for Z Fellows (optional field) and Carbon13 (required). Record on
your phone, unlisted YouTube upload, no demo — just you talking.

## Script

Hi, I'm Thomas Price — I go by T.R. I'm the founder of Temple Earth,
based in Denver.

Background: I've spent the last several years building infrastructure
software solo. A few years back I deployed a deterministic spiking-neural
engine over real connectome wiring on-chain — probably the first time
organism wiring ran as verifiable compute. That taught me something most
people miss: biology is already doing computation, and the missing piece
isn't better models — it's evidence.

So the thing I'm working on today: Temple Earth is a living cloud.
Open-hardware field nodes listen to ecosystems — passive, consent-gated,
nothing attached — and every detection becomes a witnessed,
replay-verifiable receipt. Living systems earn credit for the work they
already do. The whole protocol is open source, the receipts are public,
and anyone can recompute and verify.

What I'm brag-worthy about: I built the entire stack myself — firmware,
policy gates, federation adapters, a deterministic compute core with
bit-identical Python and TypeScript implementations. Most teams need a
year for what shipped in months.

The goal: make ecological evidence something nobody has to take on
trust — and make nature a participant in the economy, not an input to it.
