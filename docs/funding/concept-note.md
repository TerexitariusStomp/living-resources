## Title

Verifiable ecological sensing: per-reading receipts for the living cloud

## One-paragraph summary

Ecological monitoring today produces aggregate dashboards that buyers
must take on trust. We have built and open-sourced a sensing commons
where every field reading is content-addressed (SAID), merged by quorum
across independent nodes, and issued as a witnessed receipt — so a
biodiversity or carbon claim carries the evidence chain that produced
it, and anyone can replay the computation that stands behind it. The
same evidence layer runs deterministic inference over preserved
real-organism connectomes, producing replay-verifiable computational
signatures — verifiable compute that no MRV platform, sensor vendor,
or wetware company currently offers.

## The problem [ADAPT emphasis per program]

Conservation, MRV, and nature-finance buyers face an integrity crisis:
claims are only as strong as the provider's word. Carbon-market integrity
initiatives (ICVCM, VCMI), biodiversity-credit standards, and Article 6
all name measurement-verification trust as the bottleneck. Meanwhile,
sensing hardware is either research-grade-expensive or consumer-grade-
unverifiable. Nobody offers evidence that *travels with the reading*.

## What exists (proof, not promises)

- **Open-source evidence stack** (AGPL): sensing readings → SAIDs →
  quorum merge → witnessed receipts → per-contribution mutual credit.
  e2e suites, consent-gated emit lanes, structured refusals.
- **Universal field node** (EcoCell One): ~$35–45 BOM, all electronics
  on one DRC-clean PCB, orderable from Seeed today — multimodal
  (acoustic, ultrasonic, environmental, C4D), Reticulum/LXMF mesh.
- **Replay-verified compute**: LIF inference over 15 packed real-organism
  connectomes (C. elegans → 40k-neuron fly medulla). Deterministic task
  traces reproduce bit-for-bit on replay — computation that is
  self-evidencing.
- **Measured efficiency benchmark**: RAPL joule deltas across all 15
  connectomes vs dense/sparse baselines, with a degree-matched topology
  control. Published including unfavorable results — the honesty tiers
  (measured/estimated/cited) are the product.

## Why us / why now

- First mover in receipted ecological evidence; the wetware industry
  (CL1, Neuroplatform) proves market appetite for biology-adjacent
  compute while lacking any verification layer — we are that layer.
- AGPL codebase with working e2e suites and a manufacturable board —
  de-risked beyond typical pre-seed stage.
- Minimum-disruption design (passive listen-only, consent-gated
  emission) matches conservation ethics and regulatory trends.

## Non-invasive commitment

Nothing attaches to organisms, nothing enters soil, no disruptive
emission by default. All claims carry explicit evidence status —
refused framings are documented, not hidden.
