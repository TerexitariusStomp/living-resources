the living cloud

Living systems,
on the record.

A federation where plants, reefs and biofilms are providers, not resources — sensed passively, witnessed by quorum, credited per contribution. Nothing attaches to an organism, nothing enters the soil, and every write lane rides on recorded consent. What flows out is evidence: content-addressed readings, replayable compute, receipts anyone can check.

One lane of that cloud is already running — replay-verified compute over 15 real EM-derived connectomes. Deterministic simulation over preserved wiring: no organism computed your answer, and the honest scope of every other lane is written on it below.

The lane map

every lane wears its evidence tier
witnesslive This API — deterministic LIF tasks over 15 real EM-derived connectomes, every receipt replayable to an identical digest. Simulation over preserved wiring, as an additive evidence leg — not the refused organism-compute lane. lif.py · lif.ts · connectome.scm
storelive As receipts: every reading and task is content-addressed and persisted — tamper-evident commitments, not bulk storage. (The gate's organism-bound store lane is consent-gated — see below; what runs today is the evidence ledger.) /receipts · KV ledger
sensebuilt · E0 pending Passive multimodal sensing — audio, ultrasonic, environmental, camera, hydrophone front-end on one ~$35–45 node. Nothing attaches, nothing enters soil. Firmware + actors e2e-tested; field-validation gate E0 not yet run. EcoCell One · firmware/node
fleetbuilt · E0 pending The second evidence lane — multi-node fleet readings folding into quorum. Same gate: admitted only when the lane's evidence SAID lands. offers.scm · attest.scm
relaybuilt · transport Node→node mesh hops — Reticulum store-and-forward carrying only content-addressed bundles: an intent names the packet SAID it moves or it refuses. The network hop, not the biological one — hop below stays refused. rns_edge.py · lane-gate.rego
emitconsent-gated The write lane exists and is fully implemented — it rides only on joint guardian + operator consent, duty-cycled minimum power, every burst logged and receipted. EmitLane · consent.rego
castconsent-gated Broadcast lane — same joint-consent gate: guardian + operator, consent credential SAID, never a default. offers.scm · lane-gate.rego
store (organism)consent-gated Organism-bound storage — writing into living systems. Consent-gated by policy; distinct from the evidence ledger running above. lane-gate.rego
hoprefused Relay through living wiring — refused outright: consent is not evidence. E5 is the graduation path: its preserved-wiring harness already passes (15/15 relays replay bit-for-bit across two verifiers), the living half stays refused until evidence lands. lane-gate.rego · tools/e5
compute (organism)refused Living-systems compute — refused until an evidence SAID lands. What the field has published as possible is cited below — we claim the receipts, not the miracle. docs/SCIENCE.md
engineeredrefused Engineered-biology lanes — refused outright. Natural and wild systems only; no engineered substrate carries a lane here. lane-gate.rego

measured bytes · celegans → medulla · EFFICIENCY.md
At ≥10⁴ neurons, connectome legs use ~70× fewer joules than dense fp32 on the same silicon
measured RAPL · medulla 0.117 J vs 8.30 J · 3.3 ms vs 104 ms
Deterministic traces replay bit-for-bit — TS port identical to the Python engine
930/930 checks · test/diff.mjs
At 302 neurons the interpreter is ~20× slower than SIMD — reported, not hidden
measured — unfavorable cells are part of the evidence
Shuffled wiring with identical degree statistics performs identically
measured control — the win is sparsity; biology contributes the signature, not the speed

demonstrated by the field

Stressed plants emit remotely detectable, informative ultrasound — tomato ~35 clicks/h, 20–100 kHz
Khait et al., Cell 2023

Prindle et al., Nature 2015 · Humphries et al., Cell 2017 · Liu et al., Science 2017
Reef acoustic complexity tracks biodiversity and recovery

DNA archival storage reaches 215 petabytes per gram
