the living cloud

Living systems,
on the record.

A federation where plants, reefs and biofilms are providers, not resources — sensed passively, witnessed by quorum, credited per contribution. Nothing attaches to an organism, nothing enters the soil, and every write lane rides on recorded consent. What flows out is evidence: content-addressed readings, replayable compute, receipts anyone can check.

One lane of that cloud is already running — replay-verified compute over 15 real EM-derived connectomes. Deterministic simulation over preserved wiring: no organism computed your answer, and the honest scope of every other lane is written on it below.

The lane map

every lane wears its evidence tier
senselive Passive multimodal sensing — audio, ultrasonic, environmental, camera, hydrophone front-end on one ~$35–45 node. Nothing attaches, nothing enters soil. EcoCell One · firmware/node

relayresearch Signal through preserved wiring — input current propagates along real synaptic paths, the receipted spike train is the relayed output. E5 is the graduation path. docs/EXPERIMENTS.md
into-organismrefused compute / store / hop into living systems — refused. Not until an evidence SAID lands in the gate book; a refusal is a value, not silence. What the field has published as possible is cited below — we claim the receipts, not the miracle. docs/SCIENCE.md
